(01) The founder

The eye behind every frame.

Banaras Weddings is led by Retheesh P Raveendran, a leading wedding photographer and filmmaker whose work has taken him across India and the United Kingdom.

He has worked with the BBC, and it shows in how he films a wedding: watch closely, stay out of the way, and let real moments unfold on their own. Nothing is staged twice — the best moments never happen twice.

Alongside weddings he has made story-driven films of his own, which is why a Banaras Weddings film is cut like cinema rather than stitched together like a highlight reel.

From 2012 to 2021 he also worked as an entrepreneur, building and running his own ventures. Couples get an artist’s eye with a business owner’s discipline — clear plans, honest timelines and straight answers.

Banaras Weddings brings all of it together, for couples who want their day remembered the way it felt.

(02) The studio

Banaras Weddings is a wedding film studio for couples who want a film — not a slideshow of their day.

Our films have taken us from a Tirupur wedding and the lawns of Lodhi Gardens to an English country house and a Cotswolds church. The places change. The approach doesn’t: stay close, stay quiet, and let the day tell its own story.

(03) Approach

01

Observe, don’t direct

We’d rather catch a real laugh than stage a perfect one. Most of what ends up in the film happens when nobody is posing.

02

Sound is half the film

Vows, speeches, a grandparent’s blessing — we record the day’s words with care, because they’re what you’ll want to hear again.

03

Edited like cinema

Every film is cut by hand, with its own pace and its own music, so no two ever feel the same.
