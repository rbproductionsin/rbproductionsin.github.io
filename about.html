(01) The founder

The eye behind every frame.

Banaras Productions is led by Retheesh P Raveendran, a leading wedding photographer and filmmaker who has filmed more than 800 weddings across 28 countries.

He is associated with the BBC, and it shows in how he films: watch closely, stay out of the way, and let real moments unfold on their own. Nothing is staged twice — the best moments never happen twice.

It began in 2012 with Scenario Movies, his first studio, making story-driven films. Scenario Movies became Banaras Weddings, and today it is Banaras Productions — weddings, films, commercial ads, product shoots and corporate events.

Along the way he has filmed for some of the finest names in weddings, including Keeley Wedding Films, Mystic Studios, Aaron Obed Photography, Sabyasachi, Paradise Visual Media and Surya Visual Media.

Couples and brands get an artist’s eye with a business owner’s discipline — clear plans, honest timelines and straight answers, and a film remembered the way the day felt.

(02) The studio

Banaras Productions is a film studio for couples and brands who want a film — not a slideshow.

Our films have taken us from a Tirupur wedding and the lawns of Lodhi Gardens to an English country house and a Cotswolds church. The places change. The approach doesn’t: stay close, stay quiet, and let the day tell its own story.

(03) Approach

01

Observe, don’t direct

We’d rather catch a real laugh than stage a perfect one. Most of what ends up in the film happens when nobody is posing.

02

Sound is half the film

Vows, speeches, a grandparent’s blessing — we record the day’s words with care, because they’re what you’ll want to hear again.

03

Edited like cinema

Every film is cut by hand, with its own pace and its own music, so no two ever feel the same.
